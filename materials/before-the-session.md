**Subject:** Guest seminar on AI-augmented qualitative research: please bring a laptop

Hi all,

The guest seminar on AI-augmented qualitative research will include short hands-on activities, so please:

1. **Bring a laptop** (a tablet works for most activities but is fiddlier).
2. **Have an account on at least one AI chat tool** and check you can sign in: Claude (claude.ai), ChatGPT, Gemini, or Copilot. A free account is enough for every activity.
3. **Optional:** if you have a paid Claude plan, you'll be able to try some extra features (connectors to literature databases and Skills). Not required.

No software installation is needed, and no coding experience is assumed.

Please don't bring real participant data. We'll practise on fictional field notes provided in the session.
