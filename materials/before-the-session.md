# Before the session

For the course coordinator to send to students about a week ahead.

---

**Subject:** Guest seminar on 23 October: getting started with agentic AI for research. Please bring a laptop

Hi all,

The guest seminar "From Chatbot to Research Assistant" includes short hands-on activities. To get the most out of it, please:

1. **Bring a laptop and charger.**
2. **If you can, install Claude Code or Codex and check you can log in.** Both need a paid plan (Claude Pro or above, or ChatGPT Plus or above). Use the desktop app so you don't need the terminal.
3. **Optional:** if you use Claude Code, connect Consensus (or PubMed) at claude.ai/customize/connectors. We'll also set it up together in the session.

**No paid plan? No problem.** You'll pair up with someone who has one and take a role:

- **Driver:** has the tool and runs the workflow.
- **Reviewer:** reads the plan, challenges the output, checks sources, and approves or rejects.

Reviewing is real work, and it's what most of the session is about. One activity needs no AI at all.

No coding experience is assumed.

Please don't bring real participant data. We'll practise on fictional field notes and chat messages provided in the session.
