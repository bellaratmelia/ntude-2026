# Sample field notes (fictional)

These excerpts are **fictional**, written for teaching. They describe a made-up Singapore hawker food Telegram group ("Makan Kakis SG", around 2,000 members) and its spin-off Instagram account. Any resemblance to real people or groups is coincidental.

---

[FN-01] 2 Mar, 8.15pm. Long thread tonight after someone posted a photo of the old Bedok market from the 90s. At least 30 replies, mostly "my grandma used to bring me here" stories. The admin pinned it. One member said the group is "the only place my kids hear about these stalls".

[FN-02] 6 Mar, 12.30pm. A new member asked whether a char kway teow stall at a hawker centre in Ang Mo Kio is "still good". Three people replied with links to old messages in the group from 2023. One wrote "scroll up lah, we discussed already". The new member said the search bar "doesn't find anything" when she types the stall name in Chinese.

[FN-03] 14 Mar, 9.40pm. A member posted a photo of an uncle's stall with the caption "last week before he retires!!" Within an hour it had been forwarded to at least three other groups (I could see the "forwarded" tag when it came back to us). Someone asked the original poster to take the uncle's face out of the photo. They didn't reply.

[FN-04] 19 Mar, 7.05pm. The Instagram account reposted a member's review without crediting them. The member complained in the Telegram group: "I wrote it for the group, not for IG." Admin apologised and said they would "ask first next time". Several members reacted with the thumbs-up emoji; one wrote "but the IG is public what, anyone can see anyway".

[FN-05] 23 Mar, 2.20pm. Interviewed a member (pseudonym "Auntie L") at a coffee shop. She says she never uses the group's search; instead she keeps a paper notebook of stalls people recommend, and "checks the menu boards when I go". She reads the group "like the newspaper", every morning, but rarely posts.

[FN-06] 28 Mar, 10.50pm. Screenshot from 2019 resurfaced: a member's harsh review of a stall that has since closed. Someone replied "wah this one aged badly". The original reviewer, still in the group, wrote "I was young and hungry, pls delete". Admin said Telegram doesn't let them delete other people's old messages. Thread drifted into members sharing what they miss about stalls that have closed.
